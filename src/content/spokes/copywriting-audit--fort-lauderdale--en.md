## When should a Fort Lauderdale business audit its website copy?

Most Fort Lauderdale businesses commission a copywriting audit when they notice a specific problem: organic traffic declining, conversion rates dropping, or competitors outranking them for searches that used to bring in leads. But the most effective time to audit is before investing in new content production — otherwise, you risk building on a weak foundation.

A copywriting audit is especially valuable for businesses that have accumulated content over several years without a cohesive strategy. This is common among professional services firms, marine companies with large product catalogues, and home service businesses that added pages for each new service offering. The audit separates the assets worth investing in from the pages that should be consolidated or retired.

## What does the audit reveal that analytics alone cannot?

Analytics tools show you traffic and bounce rates, but they cannot tell you why a page underperforms. A copywriting audit answers the qualitative questions: Does the copy match the search intent behind your target keywords? Does it address the specific concerns Fort Lauderdale customers have? Is it structured so that AI platforms can extract and cite your answers?

We cross-reference your content against Broward County search data to identify mismatches between what your pages say and what your customers search for. The output is a scored report with a prioritised action plan — not a generic best-practices document.
