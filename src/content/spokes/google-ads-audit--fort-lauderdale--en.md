## How do you know if your Fort Lauderdale Google Ads account is wasting money?

Most Fort Lauderdale businesses we audit are losing between 20 and 40 percent of their ad spend on clicks that will never convert. The causes are consistent: broad match keywords pulling in Miami or Palm Beach traffic, missing negative keyword lists, conversion tracking that counts page views as leads, and campaigns that have not been restructured since they were launched.

If your cost-per-lead has been creeping up or your lead volume has plateaued, the problem is almost always structural. Our [free Google Ads audit](/audits/free-google-ads-check/) identifies exactly where the waste is and what to fix first.

## What does the audit process look like?

We request read-only access to your Google Ads account and run a systematic review across five dimensions: structure, spend efficiency, conversion accuracy, competitive position, and Quality Score health. The output is a scored report with a prioritised action plan — not a generic checklist, but specific recommendations tied to your Fort Lauderdale market and business model.

The audit takes five business days. There is no cost and no obligation. If you want StarkRank to implement the recommendations through ongoing [Google Ads management](/google-ads-fort-lauderdale/), we scope that as a separate engagement.

## Why is geographic precision critical for Fort Lauderdale advertisers?

Fort Lauderdale shares a metro area with Miami, and Google's default location targeting includes people "interested in" your target area — not just people physically there. This means a Fort Lauderdale plumber can end up paying for clicks from users in Hialeah or Homestead. We audit geo settings, bid adjustments by location, and search term geography to ensure your budget stays focused on Broward County.

This geographic discipline extends into [cross-channel campaigns](/ads-management-fort-lauderdale/) and [paid social targeting](/paid-social-fort-lauderdale/), where similar location bleed can inflate costs across platforms.
