## Why is reactive PR especially effective in the Miami market?

Miami generates news at a pace that few US cities match. On any given day, a record-breaking real estate transaction, a major tourism milestone, a fintech funding round, or a weather event creates media demand for expert commentary. South Florida journalists work on tight deadlines across multiple beats, and they need credible sources who can respond in hours, not days.

This high news frequency is an advantage for brands that are prepared. A reactive PR strategy turns your existing expertise into timely media placements. When a journalist at the Miami Herald needs a real estate analyst to comment on condo market data, or the South Florida Business Journal covers a new fintech regulation, the source who responds first with a credible quote earns the placement — and the authority that comes with it.

## How does reactive coverage build authority in AI search?

AI platforms build their understanding of brand authority from the breadth, recency, and consistency of media mentions. Reactive PR generates a steady stream of fresh, high-quality placements that signal to ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews that your brand is an active, authoritative voice in the Miami market.

Each reactive placement adds another data point: your brand name, your spokesperson's credentials, and your area of expertise — all attributed consistently across authoritative South Florida domains. Over time, this entity reinforcement means AI platforms are more likely to cite your business when users ask questions related to your sector in Miami.
