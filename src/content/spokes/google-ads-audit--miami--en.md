## How do you know your Miami Google Ads account is wasting money?

Most businesses running Google Ads in Miami are overspending without realising it. The symptoms are predictable: broad match keywords triggering irrelevant queries, ad groups with dozens of unrelated keywords sharing the same ad copy, and conversion tracking that either double-counts or misses actions entirely. In a market where real estate clicks can cost $30+ and legal keywords exceed $50 CPC (cost per click), even small inefficiencies add up to thousands of dollars per month in wasted PPC (pay-per-click) spend.

Our audit starts with a [free Google Ads health check](/audits/free-google-ads-check/) that surfaces the biggest issues within 48 hours. The full audit goes deeper — analysing every campaign, ad group, and keyword for structural problems, tracking gaps, and competitive blind spots.

## What does the audit process look like?

We request read-only access to your Google Ads account and run a systematic review across four layers: spend efficiency, campaign structure, tracking accuracy, and competitive positioning. For Miami accounts, we pay particular attention to geo-targeting settings (many accounts leak budget to areas outside South Florida), bilingual keyword coverage, and industry-specific benchmarks.

The output is a prioritised action plan — not a generic checklist. Each recommendation includes expected impact, implementation effort, and estimated budget recovery. If the audit reveals structural issues, our [Google Ads Management](/google-ads-miami/) team can implement the fixes directly.

## Should you audit before switching agencies or increasing budget?

Always. Increasing budget on a poorly structured account just accelerates waste. And switching agencies without an independent audit means the new team inherits problems they may not identify. An audit gives you a clear baseline, so any future [Performance Ads Management](/ads-management-miami/) engagement starts from a position of knowledge rather than guesswork.
