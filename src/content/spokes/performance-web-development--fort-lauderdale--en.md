## Why does website speed matter more in Fort Lauderdale than you think?

Fort Lauderdale businesses compete in a market where mobile usage dominates — customers search for services while commuting on I-95, sitting at the beach, or waiting at a marina. These users are on cellular connections with variable speeds, and they have zero patience for slow-loading pages. Every 100 milliseconds of added load time reduces conversion rates measurably.

The problem is compounded by the platform choices common among Broward County SMBs. Template builders and shared hosting plans impose performance ceilings that no amount of content optimisation can overcome. If your server response time is 800 milliseconds before a single asset loads, you have already lost the performance battle.

## What does a performance-first build look like?

We start by measuring your current site with both lab tools (Lighthouse, WebPageTest) and field data (Chrome UX Report) to establish a real-world baseline. The build phase focuses on static-first architecture, optimised asset delivery, and minimal JavaScript — producing sites that consistently score above 90 on Lighthouse performance.
