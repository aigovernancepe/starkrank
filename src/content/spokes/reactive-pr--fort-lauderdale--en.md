## What makes reactive PR different from planned campaigns in Fort Lauderdale?

Planned digital PR campaigns are built around original research and long-lead stories. Reactive PR is the opposite: it responds to what is already happening in the news cycle and positions your Fort Lauderdale business as the expert source. When a Sun Sentinel reporter covers a Broward County zoning decision, when marine industry news breaks during FLIBS, or when a national outlet writes about South Florida tourism trends — reactive PR ensures your brand is part of that conversation.

The advantage for Fort Lauderdale businesses is speed. Most local companies lack the media relationships and internal processes to respond before a journalist's deadline passes. We solve that by maintaining always-ready spokesperson materials and monitoring the local news cycle daily, so your commentary is delivered while the story is still being shaped.

## How do you monitor media opportunities specific to Fort Lauderdale?

We configure monitoring across three tiers: Broward County local media (Sun Sentinel, WPLG, local business journals), marine and yachting trade publications (which are headquartered or heavily focused on Fort Lauderdale), and South Florida regional outlets that cover the Greater Fort Lauderdale area distinctly from Miami-Dade. This layered approach catches opportunities that generic South Florida monitoring would miss.

When an opportunity matches your sector and expertise, we assess it against editorial fit criteria and, if approved, draft and pitch commentary within hours.
