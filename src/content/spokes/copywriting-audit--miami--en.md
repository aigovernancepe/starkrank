## Why should Miami businesses audit their existing content before creating more?

Most Miami businesses we work with have a content problem they do not recognise: their existing pages actively harm their search performance. Duplicate topic coverage cannibalises rankings, outdated service descriptions erode trust signals, and pages written before AI search lack the structural formatting needed for citations. Creating new content on a weak foundation amplifies these problems.

A copywriting audit establishes your baseline. We score every page for AI citability, E-E-A-T compliance, and conversion effectiveness, then benchmark against your Miami competitors. The output is a prioritised roadmap that tells you which pages to fix, which to consolidate, which to expand, and which to retire — before investing in new [Content Marketing](/content-miami/) or [SEO Copywriting](/copywriting-miami/) production.

## How does an AI citability assessment work for Miami content?

AI platforms cite content that meets specific structural criteria. Our citability assessment evaluates each page against the formatting patterns that ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews favour: question-phrased headings that match common prompts, direct answer paragraphs within the first sentence of each section, original data or comparisons, and clear entity identification.

For the Miami market, we also assess bilingual citability. Spanish-language AI queries about Miami are growing rapidly, and content that only exists in English cannot be cited for those prompts. We map the overlap between your content and the AI prompt patterns that our [Audience Persona Mapping](/persona-mapping-miami/) service identifies, flagging pages where minor structural changes could unlock citation visibility and pages where entirely new content is needed.

## What does an E-E-A-T evaluation reveal about Miami service pages?

Google's quality evaluator guidelines place heightened scrutiny on YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) content — exactly the categories that dominate Miami's economy: real estate, finance, healthcare, and legal services. Pages in these sectors need demonstrable Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness signals that many Miami businesses lack.

Common findings include service pages without author attribution, claims without source citations, credentials buried in footers instead of visible on content pages, and "about" pages that fail to establish entity authority. We score every page against the E-E-A-T framework and provide specific remediation steps. These findings feed directly into new copy briefs when you engage our [SEO Copywriting](/copywriting-miami/) service, ensuring new content is built to the trust standards that competitive Miami markets demand.
