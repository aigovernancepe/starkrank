## Why do Fort Lauderdale businesses need independent ROI reporting?

Most Fort Lauderdale businesses rely on platform dashboards — Google Ads reporting what Google Ads thinks it achieved, Facebook reporting its own success. These numbers are structurally optimistic. They double-count conversions, ignore assisted touches, and never show you the full cost of customer acquisition including agency fees and tool subscriptions.

Independent ROI reporting solves this by pulling data from every source, reconciling it against real business outcomes, and presenting a single view of what your marketing actually produces. For Broward County SMBs where every marketing dollar counts, this clarity is the difference between scaling profitably and funding campaigns that feel busy but deliver nothing.
