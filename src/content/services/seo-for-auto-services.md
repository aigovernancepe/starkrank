  - question: "How long does auto service SEO take to work?"
    answer: "Most auto service businesses see measurable improvements in Google Maps visibility within 60-90 days. Organic search traffic typically increases within 3-6 months. SEO compounds over time — the longer you invest, the wider the gap between you and franchise competitors who rely on brand name alone."
  - question: "How do I compete with franchise chains like Midas or Jiffy Lube?"
    answer: "Franchise chains have brand recognition, but they rarely invest in local SEO at the individual location level. Independent shops that optimize their Google Business Profile, build a strong review profile with recent work photos, and publish service-specific content consistently outrank franchise locations in local search."
  - question: "Are before/after photos really that important for SEO?"
    answer: "Yes — before/after galleries are the single most important trust signal for auto service businesses. They prove quality of work, increase time on site, reduce bounce rates, and give Google image search another way to surface your business."
  - question: "What schema markup do auto service businesses need?"
    answer: "Auto service businesses need AutomotiveBusiness schema (not AutoRepair — which is too narrow for detailing and body shops), plus Service schema for each service offered, FAQPage schema on pages with Q&A content, and LocalBusiness with areaServed for geographic targeting."
  - question: "Do I need SEO if I already get customers through word of mouth?"
    answer: "Word of mouth is powerful, but it doesn't scale and it doesn't capture the customer who just moved to your area or whose usual shop closed. Over 80% of consumers search online before choosing a local service provider — including auto services."
  - question: "What about emergency services like roadside assistance and towing?"
    answer: "Emergency auto service pages are high-intent gold mines. Someone searching 'emergency towing near me' at 11 PM needs help immediately and will call the first business that appears. We build dedicated emergency service pages that rank for these urgent, high-conversion queries."
