Most Google Ads (PPC) accounts leak budget. Irrelevant search terms trigger your ads. Conversion tracking is misconfigured or missing entirely. Bid strategies fight against poorly structured campaigns. The result: higher CPC (cost per click), lower CTR (click-through rate), and rising CPA (cost per acquisition) — you spend more and get less.

StarkRank's Google Ads Audit gives you an honest, expert assessment of your paid search performance — and the practical steps needed to fix it.

## What do we examine in a Google Ads audit?

We review every layer of your account across nine areas:

1. **Account structure** — Is the campaign and ad group architecture logical, or has it grown organically into chaos? Poor structure is the root cause of most performance problems.
2. **Keyword targeting** — Are you bidding on the right terms? We analyse match types, search term reports, and identify high-spend, low-return keywords that should be paused or restructured.
3. **Negative keywords** — The most common gap. We identify search queries that triggered your ads but have zero commercial relevance — and calculate exactly how much they cost you.
4. **Ad copy and relevance** — Are your ads compelling and relevant to the keywords they serve? We assess Quality Scores, ad strength, and test coverage.
5. **Bid strategies** — Is your bidding approach aligned with your goals? We evaluate whether manual, automated, or portfolio strategies are appropriate for your account maturity and data volume.
6. **Conversion tracking** — Is every conversion action properly tracked? We verify tag implementation, check for double-counting, and assess whether your reported numbers are accurate.
7. **Landing page experience** — Do your landing pages match the intent of the search and the promise of the ad? Misalignment kills conversion rates and inflates cost per acquisition.
8. **Audience targeting** — Are you using audience signals effectively? We review remarketing lists, customer match, and in-market audiences for missed opportunities.
9. **Competitive positioning** — Where do you stand in the auction? We analyse impression share, overlap rates, and outranking share versus your key competitors.

## What do you deliver?

The audit produces a documented report with every finding categorised by impact and effort. You receive:

- A prioritised list of issues ranked by estimated budget impact
- Specific recommendations for each finding (not generic best practices)
- Benchmark data showing how your metrics compare to industry standards
- A 30/60/90-day action plan you can implement yourself or hand to our [Google Ads Management](/services/google-ads-management/) team

We also walk you through the findings in a live call, so you can ask questions and understand the reasoning behind each recommendation.
