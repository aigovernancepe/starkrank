In 2026, the news cycle moves at machine speed. Trending topics emerge, peak, and disappear within hours. Journalists work to tighter deadlines, newsrooms are smaller, and the window for securing coverage on a breaking story is measured in hours, not days. Brands that respond fast with credible expert commentary earn top-tier placements. Brands that wait until next week are invisible.

StarkRank's Reactive PR service turns this speed into a competitive advantage. We monitor news, trends, and journalist requests in real time, draft expert commentary on your behalf, and pitch within hours — earning high-authority coverage and backlinks that proactive campaigns alone cannot achieve.

## How does reactive PR work?

Reactive PR operates on three timescales:

1. **Breaking news response** (hours) — When a story breaks in your industry, we assess relevance, draft expert commentary in your voice, and pitch to journalists covering the story. The goal: your brand is quoted as the expert source in the first wave of coverage, before competitors react.
2. **Newsjacking** (same-day) — We identify trending topics and viral conversations where your expertise adds value. We craft content angles that connect the trend to your brand's authority, then pitch before the topic peaks.
3. **Planned reactive** (scheduled) — Not all reactive PR is unpredictable. Industry events, regulatory changes, seasonal trends, and awareness days are foreseeable. We build a reactive calendar that maps predictable opportunities and prepares commentary in advance — so when the moment arrives, we are ready.

## What do you monitor?

Our monitoring covers the signals that matter for timely PR:

- **Journalist request services** — We track platforms where journalists actively seek expert sources for stories they are writing now
- **Social trend detection** — Real-time monitoring of trending topics on LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and Reddit that intersect with your industry
- **News alerts** — Breaking news in your sector, competitor announcements, and regulatory developments
- **AI platform trends** — What questions are spiking on ChatGPT and Perplexity — these indicate where public interest is moving before traditional media catches up

## Why does reactive PR earn stronger coverage?

Journalists under deadline pressure need two things: speed and credibility. Reactive PR delivers both. A brand that can provide an expert quote with supporting data within two hours of a story breaking becomes a preferred source — not just for this story, but for future coverage.

This builds a compounding advantage: each placement reinforces your entity profile with AI models, increases your chances of being cited in AI-generated answers, and establishes your brand as the go-to authority in your niche. Over time, journalists come to you proactively.

## When is reactive PR most effective?

- Your industry has a high news frequency (finance, technology, healthcare, real estate)
- You have subject matter experts who can provide credible commentary
- You want to build authority quickly — reactive PR generates results faster than any other PR approach
- You are launching into a new market and need to establish expert credibility rapidly
- Competitors are already being quoted in industry media and you are not
